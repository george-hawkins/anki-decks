This list only covers kanji where ⺅ stands on its own. When ⺅ is buried inside a larger named component, use that component's name instead and leave Mr. T out. The excluded kanji are listed at the end.

## Excluded: ⺅ is inside a named component

- change (化): 花 flower, 貨 freight, 傾 lean, 靴 shoes
- adhere (付): 符 token, 府 municipality, 腐 rot, 附 affixed
- substitute (代): 袋 sack, 貸 lend
- what (何): 荷 baggage
- night (夜): 液 fluid
- marquis (侯): 喉 throat, 候 climate
- inn (宿): 縮 shrink
- responsibility (任): 賃 fare
- protect (保): 褒 praise
- fell (伐): 閥 clique
- rag: 旅 trip, 衆 masses
- drag + rag (zombie): 派 faction, 脈 vein
- genie (Heisig's reading of the shape): 存 suppose, 在 exist
